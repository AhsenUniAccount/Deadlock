Privacy policy

DeadStats is a free community site for Deadlock statistics. This page explains, in plain English, what data the site shows, which services handle your requests, and what is stored.

1. What DeadStats shows

DeadStats displays public Deadlock match data from the community-run Deadlock API. That data includes Steam account IDs, Steam display names, avatars, match history, results and in-game statistics for any player the API has recorded, whether or not they have visited this site.

There are no accounts, sign-ins or forms on DeadStats. The only things you type are player names, account IDs and match IDs, which are used to look up data.

2. Who processes your requests

  • Deadlock API. Your browser sends searches (player names, account IDs and match IDs) directly to the Deadlock API to load statistics. That service is independent of DeadStats, and its own policies apply to the requests it receives.
  • Cloudflare. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and standard request details to deliver pages and protect the site. Requests for AI reviews and link previews are limited per IP address, and the Worker keeps operational logs for troubleshooting.
  • AI coach. When you choose Explain with AI, the site sends that match's statistics to an AI model run by Cloudflare Workers AI. Players are described only by hero name; Steam names, account IDs and avatars are not sent to the model.
  • Bot check. Before a new AI review is generated, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a real browser. It runs in the background and processes standard browser and connection signals under Cloudflare's own privacy terms.

DeadStats does not sell data, show ads, or share data with advertisers.

3. What is stored

  • No cookies or tracking. DeadStats sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and keeps nothing in your browser's storage. Loaded statistics are held in memory only until you leave or reload the page.
  • AI reviews are saved on Cloudflare for up to 180 days so the same review can be shown again without regenerating it. A saved review contains the match statistics and the generated text, identified by match ID and player slot, not by name.
  • Daily counts of new AI reviews are kept for two days to stay within the free AI allowance: one for the whole site, and one per visitor that is keyed by a one-way hash of the IP address and date, so the address itself is not stored.
  • Link previews for shared match links are cached by Cloudflare for up to a day.
  • Copy summary and Copy profile link only write to your clipboard when you press them.

4. Not affiliated with Valve

DeadStats is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Valve Corporation. Deadlock and related names and images are trademarks of Valve. Statistics come from a community API and may be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate.
