Method, confidence & sources

Scouting index v0.1

Hero-specific strength contributes 50%, rank context 30%, and recent form 20%. Supported components are reweighted when a component is missing. Fewer than 10 matching hero games, or an unavailable hero baseline, prevents a top-threat recommendation. Evidence labels describe coverage, not validated prediction accuracy.

Hero wins are shrunk toward the same-hero population using 20 prior matches. Where rank is known, both the player record and population use a matching average-match-rank band. Rank context uses a player-count percentile distribution. Three-game form is compared with the hero’s population in a matching 10-minute duration band, then shrunk with 10 baseline games.

Scouting uses completed history before this lobby and excludes the current match. Population analytics stop two hours before the lobby to avoid using overlapping unfinished games. Missing rank, history, or baselines stay unavailable. Aggregate statistics may span patches when a reliable patch window cannot be established.

Focus and builds

Focus candidates need at least 100 hero-pair observations and a shrunken lower interval above the expected hero baseline by 2 percentage points. Assignments coordinate around at most two supported targets. Whole-match matchup results describe co-occurrence, not a direct duel advantage. Manual focus choices are labeled as your overrides.

Core evidence follows the most-used qualifying published build, when its current item path can be resolved. Item candidates require at least 100 recorded purchases and 20 players. Situational candidates use opponent-conditioned purchase frequency and are not verified mechanical counters. Current-catalog and manually owned-item checks apply to every displayed item.

Feed limits

Discovery is limited to the public Watch list. The optional broadcast query provides observed KDA and souls; its server-side delay and inventory encoding are unverified. Current pressure therefore remains unavailable. A recent receipt does not prove that an observation is current.

Weights and thresholds are provisional. A two-match retrospective pilot produced precision@3 of 55.6% (random reference 50%) across three eligible team comparisons. The hero win-rate component’s Brier score was 0.294 versus 0.250 for a constant 50% estimate. This tiny, selected sample does not validate the weights or establish predictive value. The scouting index itself is not calibrated as a probability. Inspect pilot results and limitations.
